# AI Reaching into the Physical World

## The idea - Summary
- Anthropic is connecting its models to scientific databases, lab instruments, robots and other hardware, through Claude Science and an open protocol for controlling equipment. Errors or misuse could cause injury, property damage or the release of hazardous substances, immediately and irreversibly. Because parts of this are open source, others can deploy them with the safeguards removed. The work also raises fears that AI could speed up the development of dangerous biological or chemical materials.
