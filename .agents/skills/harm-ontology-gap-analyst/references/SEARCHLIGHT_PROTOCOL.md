# Searchlight Protocol & Narrative Generation

This protocol governs how a discipline-based perspective generates its inquiries, competencies, blind spots, and checkable harm findings within the Harm Ontology framework.

## 1. Roster and Competencies & Automatic Prompting
When a user specifies a perspective (e.g., *Classical Council*, *Legal Council*, *Ethical Council*, *Cognitive Council*, etc.) or invokes the skill to begin an inquiry:
- **Immediate Action:** Do not wait for manual input of questions. Proactively elicit or generate the discipline's core philosophical tradition, competencies, and blind spots.
- **Question Generation:** Automatically generate 10 to 20 structured inquiries partitioned across thematic dimensions (e.g., for the Classical Council: Habituation & Character Formation, Internal Assent & Agency, Flourishing & Natural Order, Relational Integrity).

## 2. Translating Questions into Observable Harms
An abstract philosophical or sociological question is only useful if it can be recognized in empirical material (news stories, corporate regulatory filings like SEC S-1s, or audit reports).
- **Test:** Split selected core questions into distinct, checkable statements describing actual or potential harm (e.g., cognitive atrophy, developmental stunting, epistemic dependency, somatic decay).

## 3. Operationalizing the Judging Layer
Define what an assessment requires:
- **Dimensions & Instruments:** The criteria and scales used to measure the harm.
- **Workflow & Evidence:** Required documentation, evidentiary standards, and procedural milestones.
