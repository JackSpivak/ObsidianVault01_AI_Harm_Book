## Skills, offerings and approach
- **Offerings:** executive coaching; leadership-team development; organisational work on "decision architecture" and operating rhythms; a two-hour decision workshop. (thewaywecan.com)
- **Approach:**
  - treats the leadership team as the unit of development
  - develops individuals and teams together, as complementary investments
  - uses real, consequential work as the vehicle for development
  - aims to build the client's own capacity, not dependence on the consultancy (thewaywecan.com)
- **Clients and sectors:** Fortune 500 companies, nonprofits, arts, education and government. Testimonials come from Vistaprint, Reach University, SkyFuel and EarthScope. (Whipple Effect; thewaywecan.com)
- **Reported results:** a tech team where "People who were previously silent spoke up more"; reduced conflict between marketing and engineering at a renewable-energy firm. (Whipple Effect)
- **Credentials:** MSOD, PCC, PMP. (Whipple Effect)
