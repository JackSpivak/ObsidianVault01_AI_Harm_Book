# European Data Centre Labour Investigation 2026

> [!warning] Partly verified 2026-11-09. Date corrected

The Belgium, Google and subcontracting details weren't in the text I retrieved, so they remain unverified.

## Summary
A five-month investigation by Investigate Europe and partner outlets, published 30 Oct 2026, reported labor violations at data center construction sites in Europe linked to US tech companies. Most of the affected workers were migrants.

## Key facts (with page/section)
- ✓ It was a five-month investigation covering nine countries: Sweden, Norway, Belgium, Italy, Ireland, Germany, Denmark, Finland and the Netherlands.
- ✓ **Sweden (Microsoft, Gävle):** migrant workers for the Irish contractor Benbau reported weeks of up to 60 hours and regular harassment. They also said they were forced to admit to workplace infractions they never committed.
- ✓ The Solidariska byggare union won a collective agreement worth **15 million SEK (€1.3m)** on 23 October, covering its **78 members**. (Correction: the earlier "about 70 Romanian workers" figure was wrong. The article doesn't say how many of the 78 are Romanian.)
- **Belgium (Google, Saint-Ghislain):** a Romanian technician was electrocuted. The labor inspectorate reportedly found no wrongdoing. (unverified)
- Other reported harms include injuries, tuberculosis, squalid housing and unpaid overtime. (unverified; from Follow the Money coverage)
- **Complex subcontracting** made it hard for workers to complain. (unverified; the retrieved text says only that many firms on the Gävle project lacked collective agreements)
- ✓ Microsoft and Benbau responded (see quotes). Google's response is unverified.
- **Related case, Thailand (2025):** police raided a Chinese-run data center construction site in Rayong and arrested 24 illegal migrant workers. About 400 people were reportedly living on the site. (DatacenterDynamics)

## Useful quotes
All from Investigate Europe, Mei-Ling McNamara, 30 Oct 2026:

> "Microsoft is committed to ensuring that everyone working on its behalf is treated fairly"
