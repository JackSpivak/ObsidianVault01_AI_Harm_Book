# Fleet Maull

## Summary
- Fleet Maull, PhD, CMT-P, is a prison activist and Buddhist practitioner.
- He has led The Initiation (formerly The Event), an experiential training, for more than 25 years.
- [[Sarah Barab]] is a co-trainer on the Training Team and has apprenticed under him since 2010.

## Key facts (with page/section)
- **Leads The Initiation:** "Fleet Maull, PhD has been leading The Initiation (formerly The Event) for more than 25 years." (Heart Mind Institute, The Initiation page: https://www.heartmind.co/initiation)
- **Introduction to The Event:** his "life-changing introduction to The Event while still incarcerated in 1996." (same page)
- **Apprentice:** Sarah Barab, "since 2010 I have apprenticed under Fleet Maull, (PhD, CMT-P) Dharma teacher." (https://www.sarahbarab.com/about/)
