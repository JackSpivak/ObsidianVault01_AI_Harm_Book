# Ines Manteuffel

## Summary
- A somatic therapist and bodyworker in Boulder, Colorado, in private practice since 1995. Her current practice brand is **Into the Body | Trauma Healing**; she has also used the name Renewal Healing Arts.
- Her methods: massage, Polarity Therapy, Hakomi, biodynamic craniosacral therapy, Ortho-Bionomy, Somatic Experiencing, IFS, biodynamic breathwork (BBTRS) and yoga.
- She specialises in trauma, chronic pain, head injuries, anxiety and chronic illness, and also works with pregnant and postpartum clients, babies and children.
- She taught Polarity Therapy at Boulder College of Massage Therapy (1998–2013) and was a craniosacral teaching assistant (2005–2009).
- Identity confidence: high. Her own site, her ABMP profile and the state license record share license number MT.0002872.
- Reference profile for [[Marit Vogel]].

## My interpretation
- The 1995 start date fits both "20 years" (ABMP) and "25+ years" (her site) if the pages were written in different years.
- "Boulder School of Massage Therapy" and "Boulder College of Massage Therapy" are probably the same school.
- In Colorado a registered psychotherapist is unlicensed, so her two descriptions may not conflict.
- Her methods grew from bodywork towards psychotherapy over about 30 years.
