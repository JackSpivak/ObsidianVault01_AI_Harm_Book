## GSH-premise
- **"AI's benefits are concentrated and its costs are pushed out to distant, less visible people."** GSH-001 to GSH-005 rely on this assumption. Test it; don't assume it.
	- Open weak spot: show what is *specific* to AI (opacity, speed, scale, unaccountable decisions), not just "AI makes existing injustice worse."
	- GSH-005 tests it directly: is the shell company's accountability gap specific to AI?
