## The GYROTONIC® method (context)
- Created by Juliu Horvath, a Romanian professional dancer, after injuries ended his career; he developed it to heal himself. (gyrotonic.com/about, opened)
- Developed in the 1980s; Wikipedia's infobox gives 1984, and Horvath began teaching in New York City in the early 1980s. The name comes from Greek words meaning "circling" and "stretch". (Wikipedia, opened)
- GYROTONIC® uses specialized equipment, including the Pulley Tower, "to guide, assist and challenge the exerciser". GYROKINESIS® is "practiced on a mat and chair without equipment", coordinating movement and breath. Both belong to the GYROTONIC EXPANSION SYSTEM®. (gyrotonic.com/about)
- Focus: three-dimensional spinal movement (Wikipedia). Horvath's stated aim is "to be at home in one's body". (gyrotonic.com)
- Trainers are licensed through Level 1 courses, then continuing and specialized courses. (gyrotonic.com/about)
