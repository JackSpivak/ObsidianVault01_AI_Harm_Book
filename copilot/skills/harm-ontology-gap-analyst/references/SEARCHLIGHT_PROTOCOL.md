# Searchlight Protocol & Narrative Generation

This protocol governs how a discipline-based perspective generates its inquiries, competencies, blind spots, and checkable harm findings within the Harm Ontology framework.

## 1. Roster and Competencies
Before generating questions, define the unique intellectual tradition and operational focus of the perspective (e.g., Classical virtue ethics, legal positivism, ecological systems theory, cognitive psychology). 
- **Value:** Prevents every perspective from collapsing into generic moral philosophy or ethics.
- **Output:** A defined set of competencies and blind spots.

## 2. Generating Questions (The Searchlight)
Derive structured inquiries from the perspective's core tradition. For instance, testing a Classical council yields 10 to 20 core questions partitioned across thematic dimensions:
- Habituation & Character Formation (e.g., dispositional drift, passive usage)
- Internal Assent & Agency (e.g., hollowing out judgment, locus of control)
- Flourishing, Excellence, & Natural Order (e.g., proper function, uncut sprouts, artificial constraints)
- Relational Integrity & The Harmer's Character (e.g., proxy relationships, corruption of the creator)

## 3. Translating Questions into Observable Harms
An abstract philosophical or sociological question is only useful if it can be recognized in empirical material (news stories, corporate regulatory filings like SEC S-1s, or audit reports).
- **Test:** Split a single original question into 10 distinct, checkable statements describing actual or potential harm (e.g., cognitive atrophy, developmental stunting, epistemic dependency, somatic decay).

## 4. Operationalizing the Judging Layer
Define what an assessment requires:
- **Dimensions & Instruments:** The criteria and scales used to measure the harm.
- **Workflow & Evidence:** Required documentation, evidentiary standards, and procedural milestones.
