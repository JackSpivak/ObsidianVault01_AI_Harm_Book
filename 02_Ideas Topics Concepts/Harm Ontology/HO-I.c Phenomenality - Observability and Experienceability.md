# HO-I.c — Phenomenality: Observability and Experienceability

Index: [[_Harm Ontology Bookmark Index]] · Parent: [[HO-000 Harm as Perspective-Relative Assessment|HO-000]] · Tier HO-I (core model)

## Why this is distinct
- Phenomenality is a **property**, not a branch of classes. It has three values (Phenomenal / NonPhenomenal / ConditionallyPhenomenal) and applies to two **independent** questions: can it be seen (observability), and can the victim feel it (experienceability)?

## Anticipated direction
- Observability is qualified by `observableBy`, `revealedBy` (Instrument / Trigger / Time / Audit / Disclosure) and `hiddenFrom`.
- Experienceability is relative to the patient.
- Condition may split into physical state (observable), status (observable only to whoever holds the record) and disposition (revealed only when triggered).

## Conceptual territory
- It replaces v1's `hasPhenomenologicalTransparency` (perceived vs. unperceived), which mixed the two questions. The label "phenomena" was dropped as a layer name, because it implied that harm must be experienced.

## Summary
- Causes can be phenomena: every link in the shooting chain is observable. Whether something is observable can depend on who is looking (a shadowban is visible to the platform, invisible to the user). Posthumous harm, non-sentient patients and future people are NonPhenomenal for experienceability. Unseen causes can produce felt effects, and seen causes can produce unfelt effects.

## Real-world examples
- Shadowban: ConditionallyPhenomenal (observable by the platform, hidden from the user, revealed by audit).
- Posthumous AI defamation: the act is observable to all; the effect can't be experienced by the dead person.
- A missed diagnosis (an omission): not observable as an event, but its consequence is felt.

## Why it matters to the book
- "Felt but unseen" is the signature pattern of AI harm. People know something is wrong but can't name the cause, which links to hermeneutical injustice.

## Open questions
- Ancestor and afterlife worldviews make posthumous harm ConditionallyPhenomenal. Is that a value, or a Divergence between standards? (Possibly the Ecospiritualists & Indigenous Elders council.)
