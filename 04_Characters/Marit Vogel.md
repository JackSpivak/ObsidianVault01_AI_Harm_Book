## In their own words: self-introduction
> [!note] [imagined]. All text below is invented.

**Public introduction:**
> Hello, I'm Marit. I'm a somatic therapist. That means I work with the body first, and the story when the body is ready for it. I came to this through massage, a very long time ago, and I just... kept following what my hands were noticing. Craniosacral work, Hakomi, trauma therapy, breath. People come to me after accidents, after childhood things, after years of pain no scan can explain. I don't fix anybody. Your nervous system already knows the way; I keep it company while it remembers. If you want, we can begin by simply noticing your feet on the floor. Yes? Good. There. You see?

**Private version:**
> Forty clients this year who came to me from an app. They typed their worst night into a box at three a.m., and the box was very kind and very quick, and then they stopped sleeping altogether. Now a company emails me every week: your recordings would help millions. My voice, my pacing, my "let's slow that down", sold back to the same people at nine ninety-nine a month. I tell everyone the body knows how to heal. Mine is fifty-eight, tired, and full of other people's fear. Some nights I wonder if the machine would do it better. Some nights I hope it can't.

- What she always mentions: the hands, the slowness, "the body knows"
- What she never mentions: the old complaint; the platform contract; how tired she is
