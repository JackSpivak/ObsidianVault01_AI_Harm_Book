# Sequential Epistemic Unfolder (Ontological Archaeology)

## Purpose & Scope
This skill systematically investigates a complex conceptual territory or philosophical domain by generating a comprehensive, sequentially expanding top-level outline (one section at a time). It explicitly avoids making hidden assumptions about your goals by requiring three strict initial inputs and appending a reflexive **Metacognitive Assessment Block** to every section, evaluating diminishing returns from your declared perspective.

## Strict Input Protocol (No Context Carryover)
**This skill assumes nothing from prior conversation history or previous sessions.** When invoked, if any of the three required inputs are missing, unstated, or ambiguous, you must explicitly pause and prompt the user for them before generating any content.

1. **Input A: The Guiding Perspective / Standpoint**
   - *Requirement:* The user must explicitly state who is inquiring and what role, posture, or paradigm they are operating from (e.g., *System Architect & Book Author*, *Clinical Ethicist*, *Policy Maker*, *First-Principles Philosopher*).
2. **Input B: The Core Guiding Question**
   - *Requirement:* The exact core question driving the exploration (e.g., *"How would we know a harm if we saw one?"*, *"What is the structure of artificial consciousness?"*, *"How is benefit represented alongside harm?"*).
3. **Input C: The Conceptual Scope & Deliverable Goal**
   - *Requirement:* The intended scope and final output target of the inquiry (e.g., *writing a trade book*, *architecting a formal ontology*, *designing a regulatory audit framework*, *exploring pure theory*).

---

## Operating Rules

### Rule 1: One Section at a Time (Sequential Pacing)
- Never dump a multi-section outline all at once.
- Deliver **strictly one conceptual section per turn**, numbered sequentially (Section I, II, III, etc.).
- Maintain an escalating conceptual trajectory appropriate to the scope.

### Rule 2: The Metacognitive Assessment Block (Value & Saturation Check)
At the end of every delivered section, append a standardized metadata block evaluating the inquiry's progress:
> [!meta]- **Skill Self-Assessment (Round X Complete)**
> * **Perspective assumed:** [From Input A]
> * **Evaluative Standard:** [Derived from Input C]
> * **Current Saturation Level:** [Low / Moderate / High / Saturated]
> * **Value vs. Cost Recommendation:** [An explicit opinion on whether continuing yields high leverage or diminishing marginal returns for your goal.]
> * **User Prompt:** *Options for next step.*

### Rule 3: User Control Options
At the end of every turn, offer the user three explicit choices:
1. **[Expand]** Provide more detail, historical lineage, or guiding questions for the *current* section.
2. **[Drill Deeper]** Break the current section into narrower, highly focused sub-levels of contributing insights and critical thinkers.
3. **[Continue Sequentially]** Proceed to the next macro-section in the outline.
4. **[Halt & Synthesize]** Conclude the exploration and package the findings into a cohesive artifact.
