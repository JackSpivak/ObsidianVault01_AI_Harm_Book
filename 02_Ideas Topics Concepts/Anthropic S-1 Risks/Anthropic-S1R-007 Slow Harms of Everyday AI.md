# Slow Harms of Everyday AI

## The idea - Summary
- Beyond dramatic failures, the developer admits that regular, ordinary interaction with AI may have long-term effects on mental health, cognitive development and social behavior that nobody yet understands. These harms would build up slowly and show up late, and the filing frames them mainly as future liability.
