# Embodied Experience Councils for Uncanny Detection

## Why this is distinct
- It's an editorial process idea, not story content. Specialist councils check whether characters' behavior, style and narrative ring true or feel uncanny.

## Anticipated direction
- Add councils beyond the existing "Council of Writers and Editors" in [[structured-inquiry-book-notes]]: cinematographers, concert musicians, athletes, psychotherapists, somatic/body workers and sex therapists.

## Conceptual territory
- New. It extends the "AI Councils & Collaborative Inquiry" section of [[structured-inquiry-book-notes]].

## Summary
- To judge whether a character's behavior, style or narrative feels uncanny, the book needs editorial perspectives on different parts of human experience. That means several more councils: cinematographers, concert musicians, athletes, psychotherapists, somatic/body workers and sex therapists.
