# Michael J Ramsey

## Summary
- Boulder, Colorado documentary director and cinematographer. Owner of Spoken Image, which his site says he founded in 2003.
- Works on conservation films, Indigenous-led environmental stories, and campaigns for NGOs and mission-driven companies.
- Earliest dated film credit found: *The Cave* (2008), a claymation version of Plato's allegory. Identity link to him probable, not fully confirmed.
- The author reports that he is also a long-time musician and capoeirista. No online source found for either.

## Key facts (with page/section)
- **Company:** "Founded by Michael Ramsey in 2003." The same page also says "since 2005". (michaeljramsey.com home: https://www.michaeljramsey.com/)
- **Offerings:** "Award-winning documentaries, series, and campaigns for NGOs and mission-driven companies." (same page)
- **Base and role:** owner of Spoken Image, Boulder, CO; produces conservation films and directs ad campaigns. (DCEFF: https://dceff.org/filmmaker/ramsey-michael/)
- **Current title:** Director, Documentary DP at Spoken Image and Nautilus Pictures. (Portrait Web: https://portfolio.onportrait.com/portfolio/michael-j-ramsey/about)
- ***The Cave: An Adaptation of Plato's Allegory in Clay* (2008):**
  - Premiered at the Boulder International Film Festival on 16 Feb 2008. Runs 3 minutes. Shot as "over 4,000 still photographs", lit by candlelight. (Letterboxd: https://letterboxd.com/film/the-cave-platos-allegory-in-clay/)
  - Written, produced and directed by Michael Ramsey, with claymation by John Grigsby and narration by Kristopher Hutson.
  - Festival list: top honours at the USA Film Festival (2008), plus Indie Memphis, Geneva, Edmonton, Tupelo (2009) and others. (Synkroniciti, 7 Aug 2013: https://synkroniciti.com/on-enlightenment-the-cave-an-adaptation-of-platos-allegory-in-clay-by-michael-ramsey-and-john-grigsby/)
- ***I Heard*:** commissioned by the Sierra Club for the Wilderness Act's 50th anniversary (probably 2014; *interpretation*). (https://www.michaeljramsey.com/single-project)
- ***Moving the Giants* (2015, 11 min):** director; about cloning coast redwoods and moving them north. Screened at DCEFF 2016 and the Environmental Film Festival at Yale 2016. (DCEFF; Yale EFFY interview, 28 Mar 2016: http://effy.yale.edu/blog/interview-with-michael-ramsey-of-moving-the-giants)
- ***Our Story: The Indigenous Led Fight to Protect Greater Chaco*:**
  - Co-directed with Daniel Tso; he was also co-executive producer with Brian Daniell.
  - Festival run Oct 2021 to Aug 2022, including a Durango Independent Film Festival Audience Award and the Maoriland Film Festival (NZ). (https://www.michaeljramsey.com/single-project)
- **In his own words:** said he had "worked on some pretty visible documentaries" that "have won quite a few awards" but "ultimately did very little in the way of changing policies." (Yale EFFY interview)
- **In production (2016):** a feature based on Jim Robbins's *The Man Who Planted Trees*. (DCEFF; Yale EFFY interview)
