# Harm Ontology Crosswalk — Pilot: Meta-Category V

Phase 2 pilot of [[Harm Ontology Migration Plan]]. It maps v1/v2a Meta-Category V (`harm:SystemicCyberneticHarm`), its benefit mirror (`harm:SystemicRentGain`), and the attributes its classes rely on, into [[Harm Ontology v3 - Draft Structure]].

> [!warning] Draft for review
> Every target below is a proposal. Nothing has been decided or logged. External (AIRO/DPV) matches are left blank until those files are loaded (unverified).

## Method
- Each v1/v2a element is **split into claim fragments**. Each fragment is routed by the questions in [[#Routing questions]].
- **Relation codes:** `exact` · `narrower` · `split` (one fragment of a multi-home element) · `enriches` (adds a value or property to an existing v3 element) · `std` (becomes an EvaluativeStandard) · `gap` (no v3 home yet) · `retire` (with reason) · `blocked: <question>`.
- **Narrative:** annotation to carry verbatim in Phase 3. **Primary** = the one home that gets the definition; other homes get a `skos:editorialNote` pointer.
- Inventory source: `ontology/scripts/inventory_v1_v2a.csv` (generated by `inventory.py`; v2a line numbers refer to [[Interdisciplinary_Harm_Ontology_Compendium v2a]]).

## Routing questions
1. Something that happens or exists → **Factor** kind
2. What happens to someone → **Effect** kind (+ AffectedEntity / ObjectOfHarm)
3. How things combine → **CausalAssembly** pattern or **CausalRole** property
4. A reason something counts as harm → **EvaluativeStandard**
5. Who judges, when, how → **Perspective** property / Assessment profile
6. A graded dimension → **property**
7. A legal consequence → **LegalResponse**
8. Built-in valence ("…Harm") → split into neutral kind + standard + example Assessment
9. None → **gap** or **retire**

## B. Benefit mirror (v2a Part B, V)

> [!question] Design gap, not a mapping
> v3 has no `ValueCapture` class, and under [[HO-premise No Intrinsic Harmfulness]] it may not need one. A gain is an **Effect** on a different AffectedEntity (the beneficiary), arising from the same Factor or CausalAssembly, that an Assessment from *that* party's Perspective judges `Beneficial`. The harm/benefit pair then becomes a **Divergence** (`Perspectival`) between two Assessments of one assembly. If that holds, Part B collapses into Effect kinds plus Assessments, and v2a's parent-property scheme (B2–B13) becomes unnecessary because v3 properties are already valence-neutral. **Decide before mapping the rest of Part B.**

## Findings from the pilot
- **Every class splits.** No Meta-Category V class has a single v3 home. The usual split is Factor (mechanism) + CausalAssembly (pattern) + Effect (what results) + a standard or historyNote.
- **Gaps v2a would fill:** feedback dynamics (C-1), marginal-cost scalability (C-2), named CausalAssembly patterns (coordination trap, moral hazard, adverse selection) and a possible **economic** branch of EvaluativeStandard (V-1d, V-2e).
- **Biggest decision:** the benefit mirror (VB-0). v3's valence-neutral design may absorb all of Part B.
- **Blocked rows** depend on open questions: Agent and AI systems (V-0c, V-3a, C-4), Condition split (V-3c), Assent/Judgment (V-4c), consent (VB-3), role types (C-9).
- **Routing questions held up**, but Q3 (combination) was used far more than in a legal meta-category. Q8 (built-in valence) applied to every class.
