# Underage Workers Training AI (WIRED)

> [!warning] Unverified
> These notes are based only on reposts and secondary coverage. The wired.com original wasn't retrieved, and the publication date is unconfirmed.

## Summary
WIRED reported that minors were doing AI data-labeling and content-moderation tasks on gig platforms that feed Microsoft's UHRS work platform, despite rules requiring workers to be over 18.

## Key facts (with page/section)
- Survey: 96 respondents from a Telegram group of almost 10,000 UHRS workers. About a fifth said they were under 18.
- One worker reportedly joined UHRS through Clickworker at 16 and earned **$1 to $3 a day** on Microsoft tasks.
- Age checks varied: Clickworker and Toloka reportedly relied on workers stating they were over 18, while Remotasks used face recognition against photo ID. Minors reportedly got around the checks, for example by sharing accounts within families.
- Tasks included content moderation, which could expose minors to traumatic material.
- Researchers reportedly found underage workers in Venezuela as well.
