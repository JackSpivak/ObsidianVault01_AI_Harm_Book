# Data Center Shell Companies and NDAs

> [!warning] Unverified
> These notes come from web search summaries. None of the original articles were opened. Check them before using anything in the book.

## Summary
US tech companies often use shell LLCs, code names and NDAs with local officials to hide that they're behind data center projects. They do this mostly during land deals and tax-incentive talks.

## Key facts (with page/section)
- Documents obtained by the *Washington Post* reportedly show Google used shell companies in negotiations with at least five cities where it later built data centers. (VentureBeat)
- Meta reportedly ran its Louisiana "Hyperion" site through a shell subsidiary, Laidley LLC, and used the Delaware LLCs "Winner, LLC" and "Wurldwide, LLC" in Texas. (Wikipedia: Hyperion Data Center)
- Forbes identified 30 US data center projects whose owners were hidden behind code names or anonymous LLCs until after they won tax incentives. (Forbes, 19 Aug 2021)
- Microsoft reportedly said it would stop using such NDAs with local governments. (unverified; seen only in secondary sources)
- On 29 Sep 2026, Rep. Raskin's office wrote to Amazon's CEO about the data center NDAs Amazon requires local officials to sign. (House Judiciary Democrats letter)
