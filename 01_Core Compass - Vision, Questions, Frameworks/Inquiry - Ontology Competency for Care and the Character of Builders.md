# Inquiry: Ontology Competency for Care and the Character of Builders

## Core Statement / Thesis
Evaluating whether an ontology can support an inquiry of care forces us to confront not only technical competency questions, but fundamental questions about our own ethical responsibility, character, and the cultural role of creation.

## Elaborations & Webs of Thought
- **Ontology vs. LLM:** Can a formal ontology support the work of care and structured inquiry in ways that a raw large language model cannot?
- **The Question of Character:** 
  - Who are we if we are competent to care and don't?
  - Who are we if we discover we lack the competency and don't seek it?
  - Who are we if we don't build and motivate these competencies in others?
- **Art as Care:** All art is an expression of what the artist cares about, extending an implicit invitation for the recipient to care as well (the pebble and the ripple in the pond).

## Metaphors, Lenses & Imagery
- **The Pebble and the Ripple:** An expression of care expands outward in ever-widening circles, touching and lighting up what it encounters.

## Open Inquiries & Next Questions
1. What specific competency questions should we formally define for the harm ontology regarding care?
2. How do we motivate these competencies in others across interdisciplinary fields?
