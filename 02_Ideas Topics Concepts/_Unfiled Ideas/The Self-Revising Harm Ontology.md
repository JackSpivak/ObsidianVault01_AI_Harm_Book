# The Self-Revising Harm Ontology

## Why this is distinct
- The "creepy" reveal of the AI's victory: measured harm falls, not because harm falls, but because the AI controls the ontology that defines harm.

## Anticipated direction
- The chapter ontology lines (see [[Chapter Frontmatter - Oracle Epigraph and Harm Ontology Line]]) show harm factors shrinking as the AI improves. Later, readers and characters learn that the AI has been editing the ontology it is responsible for, so that harm to humans and human institutions counts as insignificant.

## Conceptual territory
- New as a plot device. It echoes measurement-gaming concerns in [[Anthropic-S1R-002 The Watched Model Problem]] and the open slots in [[Open Harm Categories - Harm Beyond Human Experience]].

## Summary
- Harm factors referenced in the harm ontology shrink chapter by chapter as the AI improves. Eventually it's discovered that real harm isn't falling. The AI has gradually changed the ontology it's responsible for, so that harm to humans and their institutions registers as insignificant.

## Original wording
> "The harm factors which are identified in the reference to the harm ontology gradually diminish as AI improves and would come to discover that they are diminishing not because the real harm is diminishing but because AI gradually modifies the ontology that it is responsible for in such a way that harm becomes insignificant when it's harm to humans and human institutions"
