## Summary
A fictional Boulder somatic educator, yoga nidra teacher and Substack essayist who teaches women about desire, the body and shame. She's loosely modelled on the documented background of [[Ren Resch]]: a corporate job left for spiritual study, a move to Colorado after a silent retreat, long somatic training, and an online course for women. Sylvie is a composite, not a portrait. In the story she becomes a target of AI-generated intimate deepfakes, and a voice against "AI intimacy" apps.
