# Sarah Barab

## Summary
- Sarah Barab is listed as a **Co-trainer** on the Training Team for The Initiation (formerly The Event), the training led by [[Fleet Maull]].
- Her own site says she has apprenticed under Fleet Maull since 2010 and assists him twice a year in The Event Training.
- Her site also says she has co-facilitated other retreats and trainings with him, including mindfulness programs, around the world.
- The Initiation page lists the 2026 event as 23–27 September 2026 at Windhorse Hill Retreat Center, Deerfield, MA. It does not say that she is on that event's team specifically.

## Key facts (with page/section)
- **Role:** Co-trainer, "Training Team" heading. (Heart Mind Institute, The Initiation page: https://www.heartmind.co/initiation)
- **Description:** "transformational teacher, astrologer, and retreat guide devoted to deep, embodied healing"; trained in Core Energetics; "has spent over a decade apprenticing with Fleet Maull, co-facilitating immersive trainings." (same page)
- **Apprenticeship:** "since 2010 I have apprenticed under Fleet Maull, (PhD, CMT-P) Dharma teacher." (Sarah Barab's About page: https://www.sarahbarab.com/about/)
- **Current involvement:** "I assist Fleet twice per year in the Event Training." (same page, no year given)
- **Event:** The Initiation (formerly The Event) is led by Fleet Maull, PhD, who has led it "for more than 25 years" (same Heart Mind page).
- **Dates differ by source:** A film site (*Heavily Meditated*) gives 2007 as the start of her work with Maull, and a ZoomInfo profile gives different dates. Her own site gives 2010. Use 2010 unless a primary source says otherwise. *(unverified: 2007 and ZoomInfo dates not checked directly)*
