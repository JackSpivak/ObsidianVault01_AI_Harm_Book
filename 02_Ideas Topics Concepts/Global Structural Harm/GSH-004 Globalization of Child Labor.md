# GSH-004 — Globalization of child labor

Index: [[_GSH Bookmark Index]] · Root: [[GSH-000 Root Exploration]]

## Why this is distinct
This is the most concrete and documentable link between AI and harm in these domains. It runs through AI's own supply chain, not just through how AI is used.

## Anticipated direction
Possible threads: child labor in mineral supply chains for hardware, especially cobalt; possible involvement of minors in outsourced data-labeling and content-moderation work; challenging the image of "clean, weightless AI." Both supply-chain claims need sourcing before use.
