# Stakes of the Inquiry

## Why this is distinct
- It states the stakes of the inquiry at the largest scale: the future is at stake, along with everything wonderful that has not yet come to be. It is the reason the book exists, and it is separate from any one harm.

## Anticipated direction
- State the stakes in one or two sentences for the pitch, and expand them in the book's introduction.
- Test each claim: whose future, and what is "everything wonderful that has not yet come to be" in concrete terms?

## Conceptual territory
- Sets the frame for [[BK-009 Urgency, Priorities and Who Cares]] and [[BK-006 Communication Ladder - Elevator Pitch, Crowdfunding Pitch and Wisdom Pyramid]].

## Summary
- The stakes "literally couldn't be larger": the future of everything that is, plus everything wonderful not yet born.
- Stakes this large risk becoming abstract. The book should tie them to concrete harms and to specific people, communities and species.
- The scale of the stakes is also a reason for humility: a large claim needs evidence at each step.

## Real-world examples
- Existential-risk framing in AI safety, which can be checked against concrete incident data.

## Why it matters to the book
- The stakes justify the book's scale and its urgency. They also decide what a reader thinks is at risk.

## Open questions
- How is "everything wonderful that has not yet come to be" made concrete enough to assess?
- Does the scale of the stakes change which harms count as worth mitigating?
