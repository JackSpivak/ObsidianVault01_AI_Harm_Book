| 2026-11-14 | **Harm ontology redesign: the model is deliberately asymmetric in time.** Causes *converge* into an assembly that is settled (fixed, traceable through evidence). Effects *ramify* into an open, unfinished, branching future. Neither total can be instantiated (the full causal past and the full causal future are limit concepts, documented in scope notes), so there is **no `Ramification` class**. What is modelled on both sides is a bounded selection: **`Attribution`** (the factors a perspective calls "the cause") and **`Outcome`** (a cut across the ramification at a horizon). Verdicts on still-unfolding effects are provisional. Finality comes only from institutional closure (e.g. a court), never from nature. | Author: "it may be inherently true that the structure of reality, because of the irreversability of time makes our aesthetic desire for a symmetrical structure impossible. an assembly of causes produce an infinitely rammifying chain of events  - very much not a mirror image". The difference is not size (both regresses are unbounded) but modality: the past is fixed, the future is open. | [[HO-004 Criticality, Leverage and the Limits of Scope]]; [[HO-003 Causal Assembly - the Perfect Storm]] |
| 2026-11-14 | **Harm ontology redesign: scope, humility and criticality.** Every assessment records `declaredScope` and `acknowledgedExclusions`. `scopeAdequacy` defaults to Unknown and **may never be asserted as Complete**: no assessment can claim to have captured all relevant harm. Inclusion can't rest on magnitude alone. The Perspective declares `inclusionCriteria` (magnitude *and* leverage) and an `aggregationRule` (None / Sum / WorstOffFirst / PerPatientMinimum / Discounted). Elements *as assessed* (never intrinsically) can carry `sensitivity`/`leverage`, `criticality` and `predictabilityHorizon`; beyond the predictability horizon, extrapolated links take the status Unknown. | Author: "we cannot know that the scope of our assessment even assesses the relevant elements of harm.  magnitude of harm seems obvious but insufficient for inclusion.  small but pivitol harms may generate huge but unforseen downstream effects." The butterfly effect and critical points. Sensitivity is model-relative and is itself an attack vector (adversarial perturbation, low-and-slow attacks under thresholds). | [[HO-004 Criticality, Leverage and the Limits of Scope]]; HO-002 T22–T26 |
| 2026-11-14 | **Harm ontology redesign: CausalAssembly.** A cause is modelled as an assembly of participating factors: `Factor —participatesIn→ CausalAssembly` (inverse `hasMember`). Factor-level vs. assembly-level verdicts are a recognised Divergence pattern. | Author: "i see factors as \"participating in\" a cause which we now make expllicit as participating in a causal assembly." This is the "perfect storm", and an attack vector: harmless parts, harmful whole. | [[HO-003 Causal Assembly - the Perfect Storm]]; HO-002 T19–T20 |
| 2026-11-14 | **Harm ontology redesign: Outcome class.** Effect stays the single unit (one consequence among many), and a new `Outcome` is the spread of effects selected by a Perspective (`Outcome —hasEffect→ Effect`).
