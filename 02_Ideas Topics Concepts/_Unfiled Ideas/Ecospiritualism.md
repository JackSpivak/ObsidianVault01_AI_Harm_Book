# Ecospiritualism

## Why this is distinct
- A worldview that treats nature as sacred and ecological care as a spiritual duty. It offers a value frame (reverence, interconnectedness, stewardship) rather than a harm category or a technical device.

## Summary
- Ecospiritualism, also called ecospirituality, blends environmentalism with spirituality. It is an umbrella term for personal, philosophical and religious practices, not a single organised religion.
- **Core beliefs:** nature is inherently sacred rather than a resource; humans are part of the web of life, not separate from or above it; caring for the planet is a moral and spiritual obligation.
- **Expressions:** nature-based contemplation (forest bathing, outdoor meditation, seasonal rituals); "religious greening" within established faiths (Christian creation care, Islamic *khalifah*, Buddhist interdependence, Indigenous land-based traditions); ecopsychology, which links personal well-being to the health of the local environment.
