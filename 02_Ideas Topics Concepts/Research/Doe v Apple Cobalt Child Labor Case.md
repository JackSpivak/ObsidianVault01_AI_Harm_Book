# Doe v Apple Cobalt Child Labor Case

> [!warning] Unverified
> These notes come from web search summaries. Neither the court opinion nor the articles were opened.

## Summary
Plaintiffs accused Apple, Alphabet (Google), Dell, Microsoft and Tesla over child labor in cobalt mines in the Democratic Republic of Congo. In March 2024 the D.C. Circuit Court of Appeals ruled the companies weren't liable.

## Key facts (with page/section)
- Defendants: Apple, Alphabet, Dell, Microsoft, Tesla. Suppliers named in coverage (Glencore, Umicore, Huayou Cobalt) were reportedly not defendants.
- A federal district judge dismissed the case earlier, and plaintiffs appealed.
- A three-judge D.C. Circuit panel reportedly found that the companies had no more than an "ordinary buyer-seller transaction" with their suppliers. (Phrase from search summary; check against the opinion.)
- Plaintiffs' counsel, International Rights Advocates, called the ruling a setback.
- I found nothing on later developments after March 2024.
