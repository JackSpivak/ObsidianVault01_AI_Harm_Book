# Fleet Maull

## Summary
- Buddhist meditation teacher, author, executive coach and prison-reform advocate. Holds the titles Acharya (Shambhala) and Roshi (Zen Peacemaker Order).
- Indicted in 1985 for cocaine trafficking; served about 14 years in federal prison (1985–1999). From inside prison he founded Prison Dharma Network (1989, now Prison Mindfulness Institute) and the National Prison Hospice Association (1991).
- After his release: taught at Naropa (1999–2010), built consulting and coaching businesses, and founded Heart Mind Institute (online summits and courses).
- His core teaching is **Radical Responsibility**: full ownership without blame or shame, and "living from choice".
- Leads The Initiation (formerly The Event). [[Sarah Barab]] has apprenticed with him since 2010 and is a co-trainer.
- Reference profile for [[Corwin Hale]].

## Timeline
- **c. 1950:** born. *(interpretation: he graduated high school in 1968, per Late Starters Club)*
- **1960s:** grew up in a middle-class Midwest Roman Catholic family; met Buddhism and meditation in high school; disillusioned by the 1960s assassinations. (Late Starters Club 2023; MEL 2020)
- **1972:** begins mindfulness meditation. (Sounds True 2019)
- **Before 1985:** expatriate years in Peru and South America. Master's degree in psychology from Naropa (Wikipedia); a three-year clinical program in Buddhist and Western psychology (Late Starters Club). Close senior student and personal attendant of Chögyam Trungpa Rinpoche (1939–1987). (fleetmaull.com)
- **1981:** begins teaching meditation. (fleetmaull.com; Sounds True)
- **Before 1985:** smuggling trips to South America once or twice a year, as a way "to live outside the system". (MEL 2020, paraphrase)
- **1985:** indicted. Accused of leading a ring that smuggled $24 million of cocaine. (MEL 2020)
  - Sentenced under a kingpin statute. His sentence is reported as 30 years reduced to 25 (PMI 2023), 25 years without parole (Tricycle 2004), and 14.5 years (PMI Who We Are). See Conflicts.
  - Held at the U.S. Medical Center for Federal Prisoners, Springfield, Missouri. (Wikipedia; Tricycle)
- **1985–1999 (in prison):**
  - Ran a meditation group in the prison chapel twice a week for 14 years and introduced "several thousand people" to meditation. (MEL; PMI)
  - Taught school and led 12-Step work. (PMI 2023)
  - Took novice monastic vows; completed ngöndro (100,000+ prostrations); practised tonglen to work through anger. (Tricycle; Awakin)
  - Completed PhD coursework. (Late Starters Club)
- **1987:** helps start the first prison hospice program in the US, for prisoners dying of AIDS and other terminal illnesses. (Nova 2026; Wikipedia)
- **1989:** founds Prison Dharma Network from his cell, mailing books and meditation instructions to prisoners. (PMI; Wikipedia)
- **1991:** founds the National Prison Hospice Association. By 1999 there were about 70–75 prison hospice programs. (PMI; Nova)
- **1996:** introduced to The Event while still incarcerated. (Heart Mind, The Initiation page)
- **1997:** Prison Dharma Network joins the Peacemaker Community and becomes a Buddhist Peace Fellowship affiliate. (PMI)
- **1999:** released; moves to Boulder, CO. Kate Vita Pires (Crisp) becomes Prison Dharma Network's executive director. (PMI; Tricycle)
  - Starts management consulting within about six months. (Late Starters Club)
  - First post-release prison programs: Boulder County Jail, Lookout Mountain Youth Services Center, and the Federal Detention Center in Brooklyn. (MEL)
  - Nearly sent back to prison after sleeping through a call from his parole officer. (MEL)
- **1999–2010:** teaches at Naropa University. (fleetmaull.com)
- **2000:** *Dharma in Hell: The Prison Writings of Fleet Maull*. (fleetmaull.com)
- **2010:** organisation moves to Providence, RI, and is renamed Prison Mindfulness Institute; Path of Freedom programs follow in Rhode Island. (PMI)
- **2016:** PMI moves to Windhorse Hill, Deerfield, MA; Engaged Mindfulness Institute founded; first organisation certified by the International Mindfulness Teachers Association. (PMI)
- **2019:** *Radical Responsibility*; Sounds True audio program *Living with Radical Responsibility*. (fleetmaull.com; Sounds True)
- **2020:** begins Heart Mind Institute online summits, starting with one on resilience produced in about five weeks. (Late Starters Club)
- **2021:** *Mindful C.O.* (fleetmaull.com) Bridges of Peace calls it *The Resilient C.O.*; the title is unconfirmed.
- **2024:** Heart Mind Institute ranked No. 578 on the Inc. 5000. *(unverified: PRWeb blocked)*
- **2024–2025:** Neuro-Somatic Mindfulness talks on Sangha Live, including "Targeting Five Neural Networks..." (25 May 2025). (Sangha Live)
- **2026:** leading The Initiation, 23–27 Sept, Windhorse Hill. (Heart Mind)

## Accomplishments and reach (as reported by him or his organisations)
- Path of Freedom: over 1,500 volunteers trained; digital version reached over 150,000 enrolled prisoners. (Nova 2026)
- A Brown University / Rhode Island Hospital pilot found fewer positive drug screens and parole violations among participants. (Nova 2026)
- Mindfulness-Based Wellness & Resiliency (MBWR): over 3,000 correctional and public-safety professionals trained. (Nova 2026)
- Heart Mind summits: over 2 million people reached; 12,000+ people have taken his Neuro-Somatic Mindfulness courses. (Nova 2026)
- Trained about a third of Oregon's 4,500 correctional staff. (MEL 2020)
- Has led Zen Peacemaker bearing-witness retreats at Auschwitz-Birkenau for 20 years; met Pope Francis at a Buddhist-Catholic dialogue. (Heart Mind, Radical Responsibility page)
- Guest lectures: Harvard Kennedy School, Harvard Medical School, Brown, Emory, and others. (fleetmaull.com)

## Teachings and methods
- **Radical Responsibility:** "Radical Responsibility is about living from choice." (Heart Mind) Ownership, not blame or shame. Basic goodness: "There's Nothing Wrong With You" (chapter 1 title, per Bridges of Peace). Uses Karpman's Drama Triangle (Victim, Persecutor, Rescuer). Moving "above the line".
- **Book thesis:** the main obstacle is "the belief that your current situation, whatever it is, has the power to determine your future." (radicalresponsibilitybook.com)
- **Neuro-Somatic Mindfulness (NSM):** "a deeply embodied, neuroscience and trauma-informed approach to meditation." (Sangha Live)
- **Other methods:** Mindfulness-Based Emotional Intelligence (MBEI); Way of Council (certified trainer).
- **Mission:** "my life's mission is to rid society of blame and shame." (PMI 2023)
