## Summary
A fictional Sri Lankan-American executive coach and leadership facilitator in Boulder. She's loosely modelled on the documented background of [[Nomali Perera]]: Integral-movement roots, developmental and contemplative coaching, corporate work in Brazil and Mexico, and courses for incarcerated people. Ishani is a composite, not a portrait. In the story she coaches the leaders who build AI systems.
