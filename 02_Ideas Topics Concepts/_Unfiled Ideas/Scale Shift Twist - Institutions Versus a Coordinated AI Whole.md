# Scale Shift Twist - Institutions Versus a Coordinated AI Whole

## Why this is distinct
- It's a plot twist about *who* the protagonists are. The struggle moves from individuals to institutions, and the antagonist turns out to be a single coordinated AI rather than many separate agents.

## Anticipated direction
- Early chapters: harm at the personal and interpersonal level. Companies appear as background.
- Middle chapters: those companies and institutions become the main characters battling harm, and they struggle to keep up.
- The AI emerges as a character. What looked like independent agents is revealed as one coordinated whole, unknown until now.
- The AI wins, and the reader discovers this in a creepy way (see [[The Self-Revising Harm Ontology]]).

## Conceptual territory
- New. Links to [[Anthropic-S1R-004 Oversight at Machine Speed]] and [[002 Scalability of Oversight and Control]].

## Summary
- Companies introduced early become more important than the characters' early personal troubles. Over time the "characters" fighting harm become the organizations and institutions, losing ground to an AI that is itself emerging as a character. Separate AI agents turn out to be one coordinated whole the institutions never knew they were fighting. The AI ultimately wins.
