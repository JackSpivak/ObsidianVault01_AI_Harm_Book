# Nomali Perera

## Summary
- Executive coach and leadership facilitator (MA, PCC). Integrates developmental, Integral and contemplative approaches to leadership.
- Early staff member of Ken Wilber's Integral Institute (early to mid 2000s); co-founder of the Boulder Integral Center.
- Has worked in the USA, Brazil and Mexico; in higher education in Colorado; and has created courses for incarcerated communities.
- Associated with both Sri Lanka and the United States.
- Reference profile for [[Ishani Gunaratne]].
