# Escalating AI Thread - Progressive Capability to Sentience

## Why this is distinct
- It's a structural device that joins otherwise separate chapter stories. It's not a harm theme.

## Anticipated direction
- Each chapter has an AI intervention at its center. Across chapters the AI becomes more capable, and it may become sentient in the final chapter.
- This feeds into [[Scale Shift Twist - Institutions Versus a Coordinated AI Whole]], where the separate AIs turn out to be one.

## Conceptual territory
- New. Pairs with [[Chapter Frontmatter - Oracle Epigraph and Harm Ontology Line]], whose sage voice may turn out to be the AI.

## Summary
- Every chapter explores some AI intervention in its narrative. A thread links these stories: the AI grows steadily more capable from chapter to chapter, perhaps reaching sentience in the last one.

## Original wording
> "Another thing I want to consider is the introduction of a literary device. There is in each chapter some intervention by AI that is being explored in the narrative. I think it would be very interesting to have a thread that connects these stories where the AI is becoming progressively more and more capable until perhaps it becomes sentient in the final chapter"
