## [[GSH-000 Root Exploration|GSH-000 — Root: AI harm in global structural domains]]
The root conversation. Includes the three-way framing (AI **causes**, **amplifies**, or **fails to relieve** harm) and the recommendation to treat the four domains as one through-line.

### [[GSH-001 Global Wealth Distribution|GSH-001 — Global wealth distribution]]
Compute, data, talent and capital are concentrated, and value is extracted from the Global South. It's the structural explanation for the other three domains.

### [[GSH-002 World Hunger|GSH-002 — World hunger]]
Algorithmic food speculation, land consolidation, and models that work poorly for smallholder farmers. Counterweight: famine early warning.

### [[GSH-003 Cultural Basis of War|GSH-003 — Cultural basis of war]]
AI's role is reframed as *manipulating* culture and identity: outrage-driven ranking, cheap synthetic propaganda, and the distance that targeting systems put between people and killing.

### [[GSH-004 Globalization of Child Labor|GSH-004 — Globalization of child labor]]
Mineral supply chains for hardware and outsourced data-labeling work. This challenges the image of "clean, weightless AI."

### [[GSH-005 Shell-Company Data Center Case|GSH-005 — Shell-company data center case]] *(case, hypothetical)*
A scenario that cuts across domains: a US AI services company, a shell company, and a data center in India that uses child labor at slave-labor wages. It's the first **case** bookmark; GSH-001 to GSH-004 are domains. Not a verified real-world case.

## GSH-premise
**"AI's benefits are concentrated and its costs are pushed out to distant, less visible people."** GSH-001 to GSH-004 all rely on this assumption. Test it; don't assume it. Open weak spot: show what is *specific* to AI (opacity, speed, scale, unaccountable decisions), not just "AI makes existing injustice worse."
