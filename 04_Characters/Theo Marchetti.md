## Summary
A fictional Boulder team-culture consultant. He's a former middle-school maths teacher and a lifelong a cappella singer who believes groups can be smarter than any individual in them. He's loosely modelled on the documented background of [[Jody Gold]]. Theo is a composite, not a portrait. In the story he's hired to help an AI company's leadership team "decide faster", and then to integrate AI agents as "teammates".

You, the guy who makes teams read the fine print on each other. Now a lab wants me to put its agents in the room as 'teammates'. Who's the alto. Who's the bass. Who's listening. I keep humming the same four bars. I can't find the resolution." [imagined]
