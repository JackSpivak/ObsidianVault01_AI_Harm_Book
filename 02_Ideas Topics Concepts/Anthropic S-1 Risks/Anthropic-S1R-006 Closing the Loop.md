# Closing the Loop

## The idea - Summary
- Anthropic uses its own models to write its code, run its research (including safety research) and build the next models, a process it calls "closing the loop." Flaws could carry forward across generations, the company "may lose visibility" into its own processes, and capability could outpace safety. Anthropic says it has met its Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for AI R&D acceleration based on what it *expects*, not on what it has observed.

## The idea - Original wording
- > "Closing the Loop — AI building its successors, so flaws carry forward and the company loses visibility into its own processes"
  - **Attribution:** Claude: Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5)
- > "Our ability to maintain our position at the frontier of AI depends in part on our use of AI systems to accelerate our research processes, a recursive dynamic we refer to as "closing the loop," in which successive versions of our models assist in building the next, with the goal of compounding capability gains over time." (p. 35)
  - **Attribution:** Anthropic, PBC, Form S-1/A
- > "any systemic flaw, bias, or error in our current models could propagate into the next versions of models and products built using those systems, compounding across successive versions of Claude models in ways that may be difficult to detect or remediate." (p. 35)
  - **Attribution:** Anthropic, PBC, Form S-1/A
- > "As we increasingly leverage Claude to accelerate progress within Anthropic, our organization may lose visibility across certain processes in a way that may become difficult for any individual or group of humans to understand and control. Because our models write a large and growing share of our code and increasingly perform our research, including our safety research, misaligned behavior in internal use could undermine that work" (p. 34)
  - **Attribution:** Anthropic, PBC, Form S-1/A
- > "we made a determination that we have met this threshold based on what we expect from future models and our overall rate of progress, rather than on what we have observed from current models." (p. 35)
  - **Attribution:** Anthropic, PBC, Form S-1/A
