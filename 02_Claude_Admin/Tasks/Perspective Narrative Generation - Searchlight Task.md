# Perspective Narrative Generation - Searchlight Task

Status: proposed, not started. Separate from gap identification (see the gap skill, kept clean).

## Purpose
Test whether a prompt sequence can generate the narrative content needed to describe PerspectiveType instances in the Harm Ontology, one discipline or conceptual-territory council at a time.

## Method (the searchlight)
For each PerspectiveType, generate the three parts of its searchlight:
1. **What it asks:** core questions drawn from its competency and central focus (the council prompts used so far, e.g. the Classical 10 and 20 questions).
2. **What it can and cannot see:** sensitivity and blind spots (the limitations of the lens).
3. **What it returns:** the findings it can produce and the consequences or remedies those findings lead to.

## Why it matters
- It supplies the narrative (definitions, examples, history, key thinkers) that the migration needs.
- It tests whether the prompts give usable content, and whether the model can hold the search, the findings, and the consequences.

## Candidate first pilots
- A discipline-based council (for example Physics or Cognitive), so the output can be checked against its existing review note.
- Avoid the Classical Council as first pilot: its outputs are already the most developed.

## Done when
- One PerspectiveType has all three searchlight parts drafted and reviewed.
- Gaps the pilot exposes in the model are recorded separately, for the gap skill.
