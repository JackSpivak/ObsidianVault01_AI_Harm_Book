# European Data Centre Labour Investigation 2025

> [!warning] Unverified
> These notes come from web search summaries. None of the original articles were opened.

## Summary
A five-month investigation by Investigate Europe and partner outlets, published 30 Oct 2025, reported labor violations at data center construction sites in Europe linked to US tech companies. Most of the affected workers were migrants.

## Key facts (with page/section)
- Reporters interviewed workers and union representatives in nine countries.
- **Sweden (Microsoft, Gävle):** workers for the Irish contractor Benbau reported weeks of up to 60 hours and harassment. A union won a **€1.3m settlement** for about 70 Romanian workers.
- **Belgium (Google, Saint-Ghislain):** a Romanian technician was electrocuted. The labor inspectorate reportedly found no wrongdoing.
- Other reported harms include injuries, tuberculosis, squalid housing and unpaid overtime.
- **Complex subcontracting** involving many firms made it hard for workers to complain and for unions to monitor sites.
- Google and Microsoft said their contractors must comply with local labor law.
- **Related case, Thailand (2025):** police raided a Chinese-run data center construction site in Rayong and arrested 24 illegal migrant workers. About 400 people were reportedly living on the site. (DatacenterDynamics)
