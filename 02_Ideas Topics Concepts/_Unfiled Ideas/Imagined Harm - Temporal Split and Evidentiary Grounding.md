# Imagined Harm - Temporal Split and Evidentiary Grounding

## Why this is distinct
Other harm vocabularies use one "assessed harm" class for both observed injury and forecasts. This note keeps the record of harm separate from projection, then asks what grounds a projection.

## Anticipated direction
A harm ontology in which `AssessedHarm` covers only harm that has occurred or is occurring, and future-oriented claims belong to `AssessedImaginedHarm`. Each imagined-harm claim carries its evidentiary basis, vetting and horizon.

## Conceptual territory
Temporality of assessment; forecasting and modelling; evidence and vetting; the reliability of generative pipelines.

## Summary
- Assessing harm that has not yet happened is a category error. Forecasts are not records, so `AssessedHarm` is limited to past and present harm. (Paraphrase.)
- The name `AssessedImaginedHarm` keeps the full severity and scale of a projection while admitting it is modelled, not measured. (Paraphrase.)
- Imagination can be disciplined. A scientist should be able to show the model's evidentiary basis, its structure, its testing, independent vetting, and how predictions matched outcomes. That work is done once for a pipeline, not re-argued for each claim. (Paraphrase.)
- The ontology should validate the generative pipeline (perspectives and baselines), not only the isolated output. (Paraphrase.)
- Open: whether a grounding score can mean the same thing across climate, epidemiology and AI safety.

## Real-world examples
- Climate projections for multi-decade horizons, where the critique is about imaginative leaps from present proxies.
- AI existential-risk forecasts, where the question is what evidence anchors a catastrophic projection.

## Why it matters to the book
It gives the book a way to take forecasts seriously without treating them as facts, and to ask of any projected harm: what is the pipeline, what grounds it, and has it been vetted?

## Open questions
- Can a grounding score be meaningful across domains?
- How is inherited reliability from a verified pipeline recorded when a core principle is later disputed?
- Is the horizon a property of the assessment or of the perspective?
