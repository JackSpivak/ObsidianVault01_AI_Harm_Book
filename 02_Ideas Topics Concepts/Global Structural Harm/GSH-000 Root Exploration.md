# GSH-000 — Root: AI harm in global structural domains

Index: [[_GSH Bookmark Index]]

## Why this is distinct
This is the root of a new exploration: how AI harm applies to four global structural domains. The harm here is mostly systemic and spread out, not tied to particular incidents.

## Anticipated direction
The root is the place to question or revise the taxonomy itself. It's also where to test the GSH-premise, and to decide whether the four domains become one through-line in the book or separate chapters.

## Conceptual territory
This is new ground. It builds on the "economic" and "social/structural" harm categories in [[2026-10-15 - 000 — Harm & Guardrails Root Exploration]].

## Summary
- **User:** proposed discussing how AI harm applies to world hunger, wealth inequity, the cultural basis of war, and child labor.
- **Claude:** AI can **cause** harm, **amplify** existing harm, or **fail to relieve** it. Most of the real story is in amplifying and failing to relieve. The four domains share one pattern: benefits are concentrated and costs are pushed out to others. The weak spot is showing what is specific to AI.

## Original wording
- **User:** "lets discuss applicability of ai harm to domains of world hunger, global wealth distribution inequities, cultural basis of war and globalization of child labor"
- **Claude (Opus 5.5, claude-opus-5-5):** "I think most of the real story in these domains is in 2 and 3, not 1. That's the honest framing, and it's less sensational than \"AI causes famine.\""
- **Claude (Opus 5.5, claude-opus-5-5):** "Don't give these four equal weight as separate chapters. They share one underlying pattern: **AI's benefits are concentrated and its costs are pushed out to distant, less visible people.**"
