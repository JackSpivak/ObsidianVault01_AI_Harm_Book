## Summary
A fictional meditation teacher, prison-reform pioneer and transformational-training leader in his mid-seventies. He's loosely modelled on the documented pattern of [[Fleet Maull]]'s life: a long prison term, contemplative practice as survival, movements founded from inside, and a late-life entrepreneurial empire. Corwin's crime, history and secrets are invented, not Maull's. He is the mentor of [[Noa Kessler]].

## In their own words: self-introduction
> [!note] Written in the character's voice, as defined in the Voice section
> Label: [imagined] until the author makes it [canon].

**Public introduction** (keynote opener):
> "Good morning, friends. I spent thirteen years in federal prison. I tell you that first because it's the most useful thing I own. I went in angry, and I decided pretty early I didn't want to come out bitter, so I sat. Every morning, on a concrete floor, for thirteen years. From that cell we started a network that now reaches prisons in twenty countries, and a hospice movement so nobody inside has to die alone. Here's what I learned: your circumstances don't decide your future. You do. That's not a slogan. That's the only thing that kept me alive." [imagined]

**Private version** (late at night, alone in the zendo):
> "Thirteen years, and I say it like a credential now. They clap. But the scheme decided who got a house and who didn't, and I never went to find one of those families. And Danny did my extra five so I could get out and be holy. I teach ownership to guards and CEOs and I still haven't owned that. Now the platform sorts students by 'readiness'. My engineers say it's just personalisation. I've heard that before. I said it. Some mornings I think the cushion is the only honest place I have, and even there I'm rehearsing." [imagined]

- What he always mentions: the years inside; the hospice; "nobody is beyond reach". [imagined]
- What he never mentions: the co-conspirator's name; the families the scheme harmed; what the platform's AI actually does. [imagined, from Legal and shadow circumstances]
