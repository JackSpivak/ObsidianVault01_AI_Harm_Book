# Gourmet Cuisine Analogy

## The idea - Summary
- **Method (user):** Borrow analogies from other fields to show how peace gets built. Gourmet cuisine is one of the proposed fields, alongside construction, agriculture, and software engineering. (See [[Peace Can Be Built Intentionally]], line 13.)
- **Cuisine analogy (Claude, from the same note, line 17):** Mise en place (preparation before anything reaches the table); balance rather than uniformity; tasting and adjusting as you go.
