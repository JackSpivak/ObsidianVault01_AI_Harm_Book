| 2026-11-14 | **Harm ontology redesign: Assessment is a class; each instance is one judgment.** It records what is assessed, the standard, patient, standpoint, horizon, scope, chain status (actual / extrapolated), assessor and verdict. Sibling subclasses: Personal, Moral, Expert, Regulatory and Algorithmic assessments. `TheoryOfHarm` is renamed `EvaluativeStandard` ("theory of harm" kept as `skos:altLabel`). | 2026-11-14 | **Harm ontology redesign: phenomenality is a property, not a class branch.** Three values: Phenomenal / NonPhenomenal / ConditionallyPhenomenal. | 2026-11-14 | **Harm ontology redesign: Factor and CausalRole.** `Factor` is the neutral base (Act, Process, Omission, Condition, Structure, Risk). It is not "Event", because omissions, risks and structures are not events. A `CausalRole` is a thing in its own right, borne by a Factor in one specific chain and contributing to an Effect. Role types: Trigger, Enabler, Amplifier, Conduit, ProximateCause, DistalCause, ContributingCause. Roles carry their own properties (causal necessity, proximity, share, distribution, foreseeability). Properties belong where they apply: `isContinuous` on the Factor, `isPosthumous` on the Effect. | Author: "the benefit of this approach is that the roles can have appropriate properties". One factor can play different roles in different chains. Deeper reason for "Factor", added later the same day: no single entity is *the* cause of harm; many things, circumstances and interventions are contributing factors. Author: "as a foundational claim, it would be illusory to claim that a single entity is a cause of harm.." | Session 2026-11-14 |
